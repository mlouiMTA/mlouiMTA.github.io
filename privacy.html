Privacy Policy

MyTaxAccountant.io — Internal Bookkeeping & Accounting Tools
Effective date: August 12, 2026

This privacy policy covers the internal software tools operated by MyTaxAccountant.io (“the firm,” “we”) that connect to accounting platforms such as QuickBooks Online on the firm’s behalf, including the firm’s internal bookkeeping agent application.

Who uses this software

This software is an internal tool. It is used solely by the firm’s own personnel in the course of providing bookkeeping, accounting, and tax services to the firm’s clients. It is not offered to, installed by, or operated by the public.

What data the software accesses

With each client’s authorization, the software accesses accounting data held in the client’s QuickBooks Online company file — such as financial reports, transactions, customers, vendors, invoices, and related records — strictly for the purpose of performing the professional services the client has engaged the firm to provide.

How data is stored and protected

Authorization tokens are stored locally on firm-controlled equipment, protected by operating-system access controls, and are never published, sold, or transmitted to third parties. Accounting data retrieved through the software is handled under the same confidentiality obligations that govern all client records held by the firm, including obligations applicable to tax return preparers under U.S. law (26 U.S.C. §7216 and related regulations).

What we do not do

We do not sell client data. We do not use client accounting data for advertising. We do not disclose client data to third parties except as required to perform engaged services or as required by law.

Data retention and disconnection

A client may revoke the software’s access to their QuickBooks Online file at any time from Intuit’s connected-apps settings, or by asking the firm to disconnect it. Upon disconnection, the stored authorization token is revoked and removed.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to mloui@mytaxaccountant.io.
